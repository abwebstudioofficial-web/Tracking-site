Terms & Conditions

Transport Hub (tracking-site.up.railway.app and the Transport Hub Android app) is an online freight marketplace in Pakistan, run by Mirza Talal Baig, Karachi. By creating an account you agree to these terms.

  • Who can use it: shippers, carriers (truck owners), brokers and dispatchers doing business in Pakistan, and the drivers their companies add. Companies are checked by Transport Hub (CNIC, NTN, truck papers) before they can post loads or bid.
  • Loads and bids: shippers and brokers post loads; carriers, brokers and dispatchers give prices. Bids are visible to the shipper. The shipper chooses a bid; the agreement for the transport is between the shipper and the carrier. Transport Hub is the marketplace and is not the carrier of the goods.
  • Payment for the transport (freight) is made between the shipper and the carrier, as shown on their invoice.
  • Transport Hub fee: carriers, brokers and dispatchers pay a service fee for each truck trip delivered through Transport Hub (shown in the app; currently Rs 1,000 per trip). It is taken from the company's Transport Hub wallet when a trip is delivered. To bid, the wallet must hold enough for that load's fee. Transport Hub may change the fee; a new fee applies only to trips delivered after the change.
  • Wallet: money added to the wallet (by JazzCash or other methods shown in the app) is used only for Transport Hub fees. It is not a bank account and earns no profit.
  • Tracking: drivers' phones share their location while they work for a company on Transport Hub, so the company and the shipper of their current load can follow the truck.
  • Your responsibilities: give true information, follow the law (including goods, weight and road rules), and don't misuse the platform. Transport Hub may suspend accounts that give false papers, don't pay fees or harm other users.
  • Liability: Transport Hub is not responsible for loss, damage or delay of goods, which is between the shipper and the carrier, or for interruptions of internet, GPS or payment services. Our liability is limited to the fees you paid us in the last 3 months.
  • These terms follow the laws of Pakistan; courts in Karachi have jurisdiction.

Privacy Policy

  • What we collect: name, phone number, email; company details and verification papers (CNIC, NTN, truck registration); loads, bids, trips, invoices, ratings; wallet payments (amount, transaction ID, screenshots); and for drivers, the phone's location, speed, battery level and SIM check while tracking is on.
  • Why: to run the marketplace, check companies, show trucks on the map to their company and to the shipper of the current load, send alerts, take the per-trip fee and prevent fraud.
  • Who sees it: your company's owner and managers; the other company on a load you share (for example the shipper sees the truck's position during its trip); and Transport Hub admins. We do not sell your data.
  • Service providers: data is stored with Supabase; phone notifications are sent through Google Firebase; maps load from OpenStreetMap / OpenFreeMap or Google; payments are processed by the payment company you choose (for example JazzCash). They only get what they need for that service.
  • How long: as long as your account is active and as needed for accounting and the law. Ask us to delete your account at the contact below.
  • Your choices: you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Drivers can turn tracking off by signing out of the app (their company will see that tracking stopped).

Refund Policy

  • Unused wallet balance can be refunded on request, to the same JazzCash / account it came from, within 7 working days, after any fees due are taken.
  • Fees for delivered trips are not refundable, except when a trip was charged by mistake (for example a load marked delivered that never moved). Tell us within 7 days and we will return the fee to the wallet.
  • A wrong or double payment into the wallet is refunded in full once we confirm it.
  • Freight payments between shippers and carriers are agreed between them; Transport Hub doesn't hold or refund them.
  • To ask for a refund, contact us below with your company name, the amount and the transaction ID.
