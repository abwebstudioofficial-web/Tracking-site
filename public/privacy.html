Privacy policy

Ibraya Tracking website and Android app · Last updated 16 October 2026

Ibraya Tracking is a private tool used by Ibraya Logistics to see where its import/export trucks are. It is only for Ibraya Logistics staff and drivers. This page explains what it collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, and email address if you sign in with email or Google.
  • Location: your phone's precise location, speed, direction and GPS accuracy. In the Android app this is collected in the background, including when the app is closed or not in use, for as long as you are signed in to the app.
  • Device: your phone's make and model, to tell phones apart.

We do not collect contacts, photos, messages, call logs or any other data from your phone.

Why we collect it

Only to show Ibraya Logistics where company trucks are on a live map, whether they are moving or idle, and how fast they are going. We do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only the Ibraya Logistics admin account. Drivers can only see their own role. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

How it is stored

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our database provider, Supabase. Only the latest location of each driver is kept; each new position replaces the previous one, so no location history is stored.

Stopping tracking and deleting data

  • Signing out of the app stops background tracking straight away.
  • You can also turn off location for the app in your phone's settings, or uninstall it.
  • To have your account and data deleted, ask the Ibraya Logistics admin. Removing your account deletes your details and location.

Children

Ibraya Tracking is a work tool for adult employees and is not meant for children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. For questions about your data, contact the Ibraya Logistics administration.
