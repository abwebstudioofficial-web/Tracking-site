    Location tracking · لوکیشن ٹریکنگ

    Ibraya Tracking collects this phone's location and speed, even when the app is closed or not in use, so Ibraya Logistics can see where its trucks are on a live map.

    Only the Ibraya Logistics admin can see it. It is used only for tracking company trucks and is not shared or sold. Tracking stops when you sign out.
